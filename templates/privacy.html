Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when creating an account, along with the expense records you log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide solely to operate the Service: displaying your expenses, generating category breakdowns and summaries, and maintaining your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database. We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery services) solely to operate the Service, and those providers are bound to handle your data responsibly.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
